First of all, it is clear that you have done a substantial amount of technical work. The report reflects a very detailed and thorough understanding of the topic.
At the same time, I find that the report is quite dense and highly technical, which makes it a somewhat challenging read. In several places it comes across more like a technical specification or datasheet than a thesis report. While this level of technical depth is valuable, a thesis is typically written for a broader audience, and making it more accessible will increase the overall impact of your work.
In particular, I noticed a high number of abbreviations and concepts that are introduced without much prior explanation. It would help the reader if you include a short background section early in the introduction, where you briefly explain key concepts related to CAN and CAN FD before using them extensively.
It would also improve readability to adopt a more top-down approach in parts of the report — starting with a high-level overview and then gradually moving into the detailed implementation.
